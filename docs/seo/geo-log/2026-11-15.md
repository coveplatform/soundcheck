## 2. Notable observations

### Groover's dominance on Q1 and Q6
Groover returns **4+ URLs** for "best way to get honest feedback on my song" and owns "get feedback on my music before releasing" alongside Dropbox. These are the two highest-intent, bottom-of-funnel queries for MixReflect's core audience. Groover ranks because it has a large blog with many on-topic articles, strong backlink profile, and years of content age.

### "ai music feedback" blog post not yet ranking (Q4)
MixReflect published `best-ai-music-feedback-tools` in June 2026 (~5 months ago). It does not appear in the results for "ai music feedback". The SERP is dominated by an arXiv paper (AI TrackMate, NeurIPS 2024) and DropTrack's help article. This suggests either (a) the post lacks enough inbound links to gain traction, or (b) Google has not promoted it past page 1.

### "is-your-mix-good" blog post not ranking (Q2)
MixReflect has a published blog post targeting this topic but it doesn't appear for "how to know if my mix is ready to release". The top results are technical mixing guides (ProSoundWeb, audiofanzine, Soundfly) — these are well-established audio publications. Competing here requires stronger content and citation signals.

### "submithub alternatives" SERP is confused — gap opportunity
The SERP for this query is dominated by results for unrelated products (Substack, Submittable, GitHub). This is a signal that Google hasn't yet found great topical matches. MixReflect has no SubmitHub alternatives page. Creating one could rank quickly given the low competition, and "submithub alternative" is a high-intent query from exactly the audience (artists seeking curator/feedback services) that MixReflect serves.

### "how to know if my song is good" — queued post not yet written
MixReflect has a queued blog post, "Is My Song Good? An Honest Way to Tell Before You Release", that directly targets Q7. The SERP is currently populated by blog-style content on mid-authority sites (hypebot, unison.audio, yonamariemusic, secretsofsongwriting). This is an achievable ranking target once the post ships.

### "rate my track" SERP is noisy
The "rate my track" query returns results about star-rating features in iTunes/DJ software, not music feedback services. Keyword intent is ambiguous. Low priority until a dedicated landing page or blog post can target the feedback-service interpretation.
