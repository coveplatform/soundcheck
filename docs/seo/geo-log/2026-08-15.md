## Key observations

### 1. AI feedback tool space has intensified competitors
For "ai music feedback" and "rate my track", three direct competitors now dominate
results clearly: **Slapback.io** (AI feedback for producers), **GrumpyMusic.com**
(AI critic with score + written feedback), and **RateMySong.ai** (0-100 score,
segment-level breakdown). MixReflect's differentiator — AI score *plus* 5 real
human listeners — is not visible in these results. The blog post
(`/blog/best-ai-music-feedback-tools`) appeared to be positioning MixReflect
within a roundup rather than as the product itself, which may dilute ranking.

### 2. "Submithub alternatives" query is playlist-curation focused
All top results for "submithub alternatives" are about playlist pitch platforms
(Groover, PlaylistPilot, PlaylistSupply). MixReflect's `/alternatives/submithub`
page targets a different angle (pre-release feedback vs. playlist pitching), which
is why it's not competing for that term. The June audit showed pos 19.7 — likely
a ceiling given the query's intent mismatch.

### 3. "How to know if my song is good" has rich competition from content sites
This high-volume intent query is dominated by content sites (Unison, YonaMarie,
SecretsoFSongwriting) with long-form how-to posts. MixReflect has the pending blog
post "Is My Song Good? An Honest Way to Tell Before You Release" in the queue — it
has not shipped yet.

### 4. "Get feedback on my music before releasing" — tool sites, not MixReflect
SoundBetter and UnreleasedTracks appear here. MixReflect has no page directly
targeting this exact phrasing ("before releasing"). The homepage and genre pages
use "honest feedback" language but not the "before releasing" / "pre-release"
framing that matches the query intent.

## Recommendations

### Rec 1 (Priority: High) — Publish "Is My Song Good?" post immediately
The query "how to know if my song is good" is well-trafficked and answered by
generic content-farm sites with no product behind them. MixReflect can own this
with a post that (a) answers the question with authority and (b) shows the product
as the concrete solution. The post is already in the queue
(`SEO-CHECKLIST.md` § C). Ship it this week; the "is my song good" angle also
directly feeds the homepage → score-report funnel.

**Existing / new page:** new blog post at `/blog/is-my-song-good`
(target keyword: `is my song good`, `how to know if my song is good`)

### Rec 2 (Priority: High) — Create a direct product page targeting "ai music feedback" / "rate my track"
The blog roundup (`best-ai-music-feedback-tools`) isn't landing for "ai music
feedback" — competitors (Slapback, GrumpyMusic, RateMySong.ai) rank with pages
that *are* the product, not about competitors. MixReflect needs a product-first
page (e.g. `/ai-music-feedback` or strengthened homepage H1) that maps directly
to these queries. The homepage currently uses "honest music feedback" + "track
score" language but does not prominently target "AI music feedback" as a phrase.
Options: (a) add a new `/ai-music-feedback` landing page with the product
description, FAQ, and CTA; (b) or strengthen the homepage H1/subhead and JSON-LD
to include "AI music feedback" explicitly. Either move is low-effort and targets
the highest-competition slot where MixReflect has the best actual product story.

**Target queries:** `ai music feedback`, `rate my track`, `rate my song`

### Rec 3 (Priority: Medium) — Add "before releasing" / "pre-release feedback" framing to existing pages
The query "get feedback on my music before releasing" and related pre-release
intent queries return zero MixReflect results. The genre pages (`/feedback/*`) and
homepage already serve this use case but don't use the "before releasing"
phrasing. Adding it to: (a) the homepage subhead/FAQ, (b) one or more genre page
FAQs, and (c) the blog post backlog ("Why your track needs outside ears before
release") would improve coverage without new pages. This is an on-page copy tweak,
not a build item.

**Target queries:** `get feedback on my music before releasing`,
`how to know if my mix is ready to release`
