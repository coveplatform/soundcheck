## Key observations

### What's working
- `/feedback/hip-hop` ranks ~#7 for "get feedback on my music before releasing" — the genre pages are earning some organic reach. This is the only query where MixReflect has any footprint.

### What's missing
- **"ai music feedback"** is the most commercially aligned query and MixReflect has zero visibility. Competitors like Slapback.io, GrumpyMusic, and RateMySong.ai occupy most results. The checklist notes a blog post "Best AI Tools for Music Feedback in 2026" was published June 24 — it has not yet surfaced in this query's top 10, possibly due to domain authority or indexing lag.
- **"how to know if my song is good"** and **"how to know if my mix is ready to release"** are high-intent self-evaluation queries. The published posts `is-your-mix-good` and the planned "Is My Song Good?" post (⏳ queue) are exactly right for these, but neither is ranking yet.
- **"submithub alternatives"** returns zero MixReflect results despite MixReflect having `/alternatives/*` pages. The SEO checklist notes `/alternatives` hub and `splice`/`distrokid`/`soundcloud` alternatives pages are still pending — that gap is costing us on a navigational-intent query that surfaces in AI answer boxes.
- **"rate my track"** is dominated by `ratemysong.ai` and similar single-purpose AI raters. MixReflect's combined AI + human panel is a differentiated offer here, but there is no page or blog post targeting this exact phrase.

## Recommendations

### 1. Prioritise the "Is My Song Good?" blog post (⏳ queue → urgent)
This post directly targets two unranked queries: "how to know if my song is good" and (with internal linking to the existing `is-your-mix-good` post) "how to know if my mix is ready to release". The SEO checklist already has it queued with target keyword `is my song good`. Ship it next — it maps to high-intent bottom-funnel traffic where MixReflect's offer (instant AI score + real listeners) is the natural CTA.

### 2. Create a "SubmitHub alternatives" comparison page at `/alternatives/submithub`
"Submithub alternatives" has 6 competing pages in the top 10 — it is a well-established informational query. MixReflect is not a playlist-submission tool like SubmitHub, but honest feedback *before* submitting is a clear upstream need. A page at `/alternatives/submithub` framing MixReflect as "get real listener feedback before spending credits on SubmitHub" fills a gap competitors leave and ties into the existing `/alternatives/*` architecture. This is also an ideal AI answer-box candidate since the query is already answer-intent.

### 3. Add an `/llms.txt` file and ensure the AI feedback blog post is self-quotable
The checklist flags `/llms.txt` as pending (Section E). LLMs index pages that have clear, standalone definition paragraphs. The June blog post "Best AI Tools for Music Feedback in 2026" should have its opening paragraph rewritten to be a self-contained 2-3 sentence answer starting with "MixReflect is…" so that if the page is cited by an LLM, the citation is a useful branded snippet. Add `/llms.txt` simultaneously so crawlers like GPTBot and PerplexityBot can surface the product description directly.
